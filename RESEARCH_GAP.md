# Research Gap and Contribution Assessment

## Purpose
Identify a defensible research question before implementing a claimed contribution.

## Literature review table
For each relevant paper, record:
- Full citation and DOI/URL
- Dataset and task
- Model and baseline
- Split protocol
- Metrics and reported uncertainty
- Explainability approach
- Limitations acknowledged by authors
- Relevance to the proposed question

## Candidate gap areas
Treat these as ideas to validate, not established novelty:
- Generalization across institutions or acquisition settings.
- Calibration and uncertainty under distribution shift.
- Robustness to image quality variation or common corruptions.
- Explainability stability and faithfulness.
- Data-efficient learning under limited labels.
- Lightweight inference under resource constraints.

## Gap validation questions
1. Has the proposed idea already been tested on the same task and datasets?
2. Does it improve a meaningful outcome over strong baselines?
3. Is the comparison fair and reproducible?
4. Is the dataset sufficiently independent to support the claim?
5. Can the claimed improvement be separated from preprocessing or tuning differences?
6. Are the limitations and negative results reported?
